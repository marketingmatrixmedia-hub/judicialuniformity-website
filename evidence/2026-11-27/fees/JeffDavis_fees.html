 IN THE MAGISTRATE COURT OF JEFF DAVIS COUNTY STATE OF GEORGIA 

 FEE SCHEDULE Effective JULY 01, 2024


 CIVIL ACTIONS 


  1.  Statement of Claim $ 105.00 for 1 defendant                                                                   and $50.00 for each additional defendant.
  2.  Summons of Dispossessory $ 105.00*  ONE DEFENDANT
  3.  Garnishment $ 99.00
  4.  Foreclosure of Personal Property $ 105.00*
  5.  Trover $ 105.00
  6.  Abandoned Motor Vehicle $45.50 
  7.  Interrogatories on a Jeff Davis Magistrate case $ 10.00  + Cert. mail fee          or $60.00 with service.
  8.  Interrogatories on a non-Jeff Davis Magistrate case $ 105.00* 
  9.  Fifa - Issue two separate checks(Payable to Magistrate Court) $ 4.00  Record (Payable to Superior Court) $ 25.00  
  10. Cancel (Payable to Superior Court) 
  11. $ 25.00  Alias - issued when original is lost $ 4.00
  12.  Nulla Bona $ 25.00  search Fee - payable to JDSO $ 20.00 
  13.  Subpoenas $ 1.00  Sheriff Service fee for subpoena $ 20.00 
  14.  Copies (per page) $ .50 
  15.  Certified or Exemplified copy – 1st page $ 2.50  Additional pages $ .50 
  16.  2nd Original $ 2.50 * Includes one Sheriff’s service fee of $50.00 .  A service fee shall be collected for each defendant listed on a case. 


CRIMINAL ACTIONS 


  1.  Bad Check Processing $ 20.00 per check
  2.  Pre-Warrant Application $ 20.00  
  3. Allegation of Family Violence WAIVED 
  4.  Warrant Dismissal - Felony $110.00  Misdemeanor $ 55.00
  5.  Good Behavior Bond $20.00  

Method of Payment

The Magistrate Court accepts cash, check, debit card  and money orders.                            All debit and credit card transactions will occur a small processing fee.
